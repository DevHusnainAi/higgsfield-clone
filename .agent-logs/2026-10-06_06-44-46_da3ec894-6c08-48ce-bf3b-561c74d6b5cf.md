## 1. Research findings

**How Higgsfield's UX works today**
- **Model first:** you pick one of 30+ models, set resolution, duration and camera, then generate and refine. The prompt comes after the settings.
- **Presets:** 50+ camera motion presets, plus image-to-video and start/end frames. Powerful, but you have to know what you want before typing.
- **Cost display:** the total cost appears on the Generate button, but only when you generate several outputs at once (batch).
- **Stuck jobs:** the help centre treats them as normal. You're told to contact support only after 24h stuck in the queue or 10h in processing.

**Limitations and complaints**

| Problem | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Credits run out fast and unclearly | Premium models cost 60–90 credits per 4s clip, so a starter plan lasts 3–5 days. Editing a video costs more than generating it. |
| Credits expire | Monthly credits don't roll over and top-ups expire after about 90 days. One user lost about 1,127 credits at renewal. |
| "Unlimited" isn't unlimited | 7-day windows on new models, one job at a time, a "fair-use pause", and a "battery" you pay to recharge. Heavy users report bans. |
| Failure loops | Generations fail or stall, and flaws like duplicated characters force paid regenerations. A Reddit analysis, which I only saw quoted second-hand, claims "80% fail or stall". Failed jobs are refunded, but users don't trust it. |
| Misleading upsell prompts | "Upgrade to use this feature" shown for features already in the user's plan. |
| Billing and support | Charged after cancelling, narrow refund rules, email-only support with 36–48h replies. |

The reviews disagree on the overall score: Trustpilot itself shows 4.0/5 across about 4,800 reviews, while review blogs cite 3.2. The complaints cluster on billing and credits, not output quality. I couldn't reach Reddit threads directly; the Reddit material came through secondary write-ups.

**What "intent-first" means here, as a direct answer to those complaints**
1. **Prompt box first.** The only control at the start is a text box. No model picker up front.
2. **Settings appear from the prompt.** Typing "slow dolly-in, 35mm, night" brings up editable chips: video, camera move, aspect ratio. The model is chosen automatically and can be changed.
3. **Exact cost before every run.** Shown on the Generate button for every generation, not just batches. Out of credits means the button says so; there are no surprise upgrade walls.
4. **Honest generation states.** Queued → generating → done, or failed with credits returned (stated in the UI). Status messages follow the real stage, retry keeps the same settings, and a timeout is shown instead of a spinner that never ends.
5. **Persistent history sidebar.** Every run is saved with its prompt, settings, cost and status, including failures and refunds.
